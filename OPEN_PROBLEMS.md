# Open Problems: Cosmic-String-Signals

This document catalogs open problems, unresolved challenges, and improvement areas for the **Cosmic-String-Signals** 3D cosmological wake simulation and matched-filter signal detection pipeline.

---

## 1. Algorithmic & Implementation Problems

- **Full 3D FFT Matched Filtering**
  - **Problem**: Extending the matched-filter pipeline from 1D unfolded correlation (`np.correlate`) and 2D slice convolution (`scipy.signal.convolve2d`) to full 3D FFT-based volumetric cross-correlation across arbitrary $SO(3)$ orientations.
  - **Context**: 1D and 2D slice projections discard out-of-plane coherence in the 3D data cube. A 3D matched filter in Fourier space is required for optimal signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) recovery.
- **Convex Hull Membership Query Optimization**
  - **Problem**: Replacing the vertex-stability hull membership test with an analytical half-space inequality test for 6-vertex wake wedge geometries.
  - **Context**: Evaluating vertex stability for every grid point in high-resolution 3D lattices ($N^3$ voxels) is a major CPU bottleneck during wake temperature assignment.
- **Native Non-Gaussian 21cm Noise Generation**
  - **Problem**: Direct algorithmic integration of non-Gaussian cosmological 21cm background noise generators (e.g., interfacing with `21cmFAST` or log-normal density realizations) rather than relying on pre-computed slices or Gaussian approximations.

---

## 2. Bugs & Unresolved Issues

- **Redshift-Space Discretization Artifacts**
  - **Problem**: Mapping physical coordinates (Mpc) to redshift space $d(z)$ on a discrete grid causes boundary aliasing and voxel volume distortion at high redshifts.
  - **Status**: Requires conservative voxel re-sampling or sub-grid density weighting to prevent artificial edge ringing in convolution filters.

---

## 3. Theoretical & Scientific Problems

- **Curved and Oscillatory Cosmic String Trajectories**
  - **Problem**: Modeling wake geometries generated by cosmic string loops or strings with transverse oscillations, rather than assuming rigid, straight string segments moving at constant velocity.
  - **Context**: Realistic cosmological strings exhibit Kelvin-Helmholtz instabilities and curvature, producing non-planar shock fronts and complex internal temperature gradients.
- **Dynamic Lyman-alpha and Collisional Coupling ($x_c, x_\alpha$)**
  - **Problem**: Incorporating redshift-dependent evolution for the spin-temperature coupling coefficients ($x_c$ for collisions, $x_\alpha$ for Wouthuysen-Field coupling) within the wake's differential brightness temperature equation $\delta T_b(\nu)$.

---

## 4. Code Maintenance & Refactoring Opportunities

- **Decomposing `Cosmic String Extraction Statistics.py`**

Core pipeline workflows should be migrated into `cosmic_string_signals/pipeline.py` and `cosmic_string_signals/stats.py`.
